## 1.18.0

Visits instead of the "I was here" toggle, undiscovered places and hexagons on the map, tags removed.

### Minor Changes

- Visits: "I was here" is one tap without asking for your location, once a day, as often as you like
- Visits: after a check-in, three quick questions about the place and "What can you do here?"
- Place page: "How it is now" and "Can / Cannot" from the visitors' answers
- Place page: who was here, the last visit and the count of visits; visits in the "Activity log"
- Visits: photos uploaded on the day of a visit belong to it and bring experience
- Map: undiscovered places from OpenStreetMap; whoever adds one first gets 15 extra experience
- Map: zoomed out, places are counted in hexagons, bluer where you have been
- Map: a place's name shows on hovering its marker
- Profile: the "Visits" tab is a timeline of visits
- Photos: a place's gallery is grouped by year
- Recommendations: "For you" follows the categories and features of the places you like and visit
- Achievements: new ones for visits, revisits and winter trips
- Tags removed; old tag links open the list without the tag
- SEO: better place descriptions for search engines, a cleaner sitemap
- Optimized the map and saving the answers
