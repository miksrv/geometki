Geometki is a free, non-commercial project made by its own members. These rules keep the site comfortable and safe for everyone: for those who add places and for those who travel to them. We have tried to write them in plain words.

By using the site you agree to these rules. Version of 12 November 2026.

## In short

- Your photos and texts stay yours. The site only shows them.
- Publish what you have shot and written yourself, or what you have the right to publish.
- Be polite, do not advertise and do not publish other people's personal data.
- Play fair: a visit counts for those who have really been there.
- We keep only what the site needs to work, and we sell it to no one.
- When exploring places, leave them as you found them.

## Taking part

You can look at the map, the places and the photos without an account. To add places, upload photos, check in, rate and comment, sign in — by email or with VK or Yandex.

- One person, one account. Accounts in the name of another person or an organisation are not allowed.
- You are responsible for your account and everything published from it. Do not share access to it.
- Everyone can see the name and the avatar of your profile. Do not put there what you do not want to share.

## Your photos and texts

**You keep the copyright.** The photos you upload and the texts you write belong to you, not to the site. Every photo shows its author.

By publishing, you allow Geometki to store your material and show it on the site: on the place page, in the feed, on the map, in link previews. The permission is free and non-exclusive: you can still publish, sell and use your pictures anywhere you like. The site does not sell your photos or pass them on to anyone.

If you delete a photo, the site stops showing it. Copies may remain for a while in backups and in search engine caches, which is beyond our control.

**Place descriptions are written together.** Other members may add to a place card and correct it: refine the coordinates, add facts, fix the description. By publishing a description you agree that it will be improved. The history of edits is kept.

**Other people's material.** If you want to use someone else's photo from the site, ask its author. Some photos come from open sources such as Wikimedia Commons: they show their author and licence, and you may use them under that licence.

## What to publish

- **Real places** that can be visited: natural, historical, architectural or simply beautiful. Check the coordinates — people will look for the place by them.
- **Your own photos** of the place itself. People are not needed in the picture; if strangers got into the frame, better pick another shot.
- **In your own words.** Do not copy texts from other sites. If you retell facts from a book or an article, name the source.
- **What is true now.** If a place has changed — demolished, closed, fenced off — fix the description or say so in the comments.

## What not to do

- Publish other people's photos and texts without their author's permission.
- Post advertising, spam and links unrelated to the place.
- Insult other members, stir up hatred, publish obscene content or anything that breaks the law.
- Publish other people's personal data: phone numbers, addresses, close-up photos of people without their consent.
- Add made-up places and deliberately wrong coordinates.
- Cheat in the game: check in without being there, fake the data of photos, play from several accounts.
- Disrupt the site: hack it, flood it with requests, download its material in bulk with programs.

## Fair play

The game rests on trust: geopoints go to those who have really made it to the place. A visit is confirmed by a check-in on the spot or by a photo taken there. If we see visits checked in dishonestly, we may cancel them and take the geopoints back, and for repeated cheating remove the member from the rating.

## Moderation

The site is run by volunteers, and we do not check every post in advance. We may correct, hide or delete material that breaks these rules and block the account of the one who broke them. We usually warn first, but act at once on obvious spam and breaches of the law.

Seen a breach? Say so in the comments of the place or write to [greenexp.ru@gmail.com](mailto:greenexp.ru@gmail.com).

## Personal data

We keep only what the site needs to work:

- **your email** — to sign in and for notifications;
- **your name and avatar** — the ones you set yourself or that came from VK or Yandex when you signed in;
- **your actions on the site** — places, photos, visits, ratings, comments: they make up the feed, the experience and the rating.

**Location.** The site asks for it only when you press a button yourself: "My location" on the map, sorting by distance, the "I was here" check-in. The coordinates of a visit are compared with those of the place and are not stored — only the fact of the visit is.

**Photos.** From an uploaded picture the site takes the time and the place it was shot, to count the visit. The photos themselves are published without this data.

**Emails.** We send notifications and a weekly digest. You can turn any of them off in your profile settings or by the link in the email itself.

**Cookies and statistics.** The site uses cookies to remember your sign-in and settings, and collects anonymous visit statistics (Yandex Metrica, Google Analytics).

We do not sell your data or pass it on to third parties. To delete your account and data, write to [greenexp.ru@gmail.com](mailto:greenexp.ru@gmail.com) from the email it is linked to.

## Responsibility

Information about places is added by members, so it may be inaccurate or out of date. Before a trip, check whether the place is open, whether you can get there and whether you need a permit.

- Many places — abandoned buildings, caves, cliffs, waters — are dangerous. You are responsible for your own safety and for obeying the law.
- A mark on the map does not mean you may go in. Respect private property, fences, bans and the rules of nature reserves.
- The site is free and provided "as is": we do our best to keep it running, but cannot guarantee it.

## If your rights are infringed

If your photo or text has been published on the site without your permission, write to [greenexp.ru@gmail.com](mailto:greenexp.ru@gmail.com): give the link to the page and what proves your authorship. We will look into it and remove the material if your rights have indeed been infringed.

## Changes to the rules

The rules may change along with the site. A new version is published on this page with a new date. We will announce important changes on the site in advance.

## The explorer's code

These are not rules of the site but what we ask of everyone who travels to geotags. Our shared task is to keep these places for those who come after us.

- **Leave no litter.** Take everything you brought with you. And if you can, take other people's too.
- **Break nothing, take nothing.** Not a brick from a manor, not a stone from a cave, not a plant from a reserve.
- **Leave no writing.** The best trace is a photo on the site, not on a wall.
- **Fire only where it is allowed.** Use old fire pits and put the fire out before you leave.
- **Respect silence and wildlife.** Do not disturb animals and birds, do not make noise where people live.
- **Respect bans.** Private land, a closed site, a protected nature area — the owners' rules matter more than a great shot.
- **Take care of yourself.** Tell your family where you are going, take water, a charged phone and a torch. Better not to enter unsafe buildings and unknown caves.
