Geometki is more than a map of interesting places. It is a game: you travel to real places, confirm that you were there and compete with other explorers. No apps and no special gear — a phone is all you need.

## Four words of the game

- **A geotag** — an interesting place on the map: a manor, a waterfall, a monument.
- **Exploring** — visiting a geotag. Whoever travels to geotags is an explorer.
- **Geopoints** — the points of a season: they are given for explored geotags.
- **A discovery** — a new place you were the first to add to the map.

## The whole game in a minute

1. **Pick a geotag** — an abandoned manor, a waterfall, a monument round the corner.
2. **Go there.**
3. **Check in** — with the "I was here" button on the spot or with a photo.

Every explored geotag brings you geopoints and moves you up the season's rating. Everything else is detail.

## Step 1. Pick a geotag

Open the map or the "Game" section: it shows the geotags near you and how many geopoints each one gives. The rule is simple — the harder a geotag is to reach, the more it is worth. A monument in the city centre is worth less than a cave you have to hike to.

There is another way to earn more: **get there before the others**. The first person to explore a geotag in a season gets twice the geopoints, the next four get one and a half times as many.

## Step 2. Go there

This is the game itself. The geopoints are just a reason to finally get to the place you have been meaning to see.

## Step 3. Confirm your visit

There are two ways — use whichever suits you.

**The "I was here" button.** Press it on the place's page once you get there and let the browser find where you are. The site compares it with the place — and that is all. Your coordinates are stored nowhere.

**A photo.** If there is no connection on the spot, just take a photo with your phone's location turned on and upload it at home. The site sees where and when the photo was taken and counts the visit. A photo brings a small bonus: it also shows the place to others.

The photo should show the place itself. No people, signs or numbers are needed.

## What you get

- **Geopoints** — for every new geotag. A geotag counts once a season, so exploring new ones pays more than returning to familiar ones.
- **A place in the rating** — overall, in your region and in your town. Being the first in the world is hard; in your own district it is quite possible.
- **The season medal** — when a season ends, everyone who took part gets its medal: the top three get gold, silver and bronze, everyone else a keepsake one. The medal appears on your profile and stays for good. Some seasons also have special awards that can be earned only on certain days.
- **Experience and level** — they grow as before and are never reset.

## Seasons

The game runs in seasons. Each season has its own name, its own start and end dates and its own medals — a new season is announced in advance, and its dates are on the game page. Geopoints live only in their season: when a new one starts, everyone's count begins from zero and anyone can be the first again. What you earned is not lost — it stays in the results of the past season, along with your awards.

Did not manage to upload a photo before the season ended? No problem: photos taken within the season are accepted for a week after it.

## Quests

A quest is a walk and a game of an hour or two: several places close to each other, joined by one theme. Quests have a section of their own on the site. Open the quest's page and press "Start the quest" — it never starts by itself. Then at each point you press "I am here" and walk on to the next one. There are four kinds of quests:

- **Open** — every point is shown at once, in any order.
- **Step by step** — only the next point is shown. Confirm it and a new one opens.
- **Riddles** — a hint instead of a point. Find the place yourself and confirm that you are near it.
- **Photo quest** — a photo of the place instead of a point. Recognise it, find it and confirm that you are near it.

Quests live apart from seasons: a quest is completed once, whenever you like. Each of its points counts as an ordinary explored geotag and brings geopoints of the season, and a completed quest gives you experience: 20 for each point, at least 100. Until the quest is completed, you get experience only for the visits. You can take a quest in several goes: the points you have checked in at are kept.

## Exploring and discovering

These are two different things here. To **explore** a place is to be there: check in or bring a photo. To **discover** a place is to be the first to add it to the map.

The map has translucent markers — interesting objects nobody has added to the site yet. Get to such a place, add it with your own photo — and you get a discoverer's bonus on top of the usual geopoints.

## Fair play

There are no prizes — only the rating and the joy of the trips. So there is one rule: confirm only the geotags you have really been to. Otherwise the whole point is lost.

## Frequent questions

**Do I need to install anything?** No, everything works on the site.

**I pressed "I was here" at home. What happens?** The visit is marked, but it does not count in the season. Once you are at the place, press "I am here now" under the button — or upload a photo taken there.

**I visited this place before the season started. Does it count?** No, a season starts from a clean sheet. But it is a good reason to go again.

**Will a photo without location do?** No: without it the site cannot tell where the photo was taken. Turn location on in your camera settings, or confirm the visit with the button.

**Can I play alone?** Of course. You can compete with yourself too — for example, try to explore your whole district.
